MediaFlowSwift vs habitat flow: Which One Fits You?

Both check a card's copy before you format it, and both have a free trial. habitat flow is a lighter, cheaper tool focused on the offload itself, with a free tier and a live iPhone companion. MediaFlowSwift covers more of the pipeline after the copy: categorizing, reviewing, archiving, reporting and publishing — at a higher starting price.

What does each app actually verify?

MediaFlowSwift computes a SHA-256 checksum for every file at import, then reads the whole copy back and compares it, by default — the hash function set out in the US government's Secure Hash Standard. It checks again, from scratch, right before Clear Card deletes anything from the card. habitat flow computes an xxHash64 checksum for every file and leaves an ASC Media Hash List report with the footage, [per habitat flow's own site](https://habitat-flow.com). xxHash64 is a fast, widely used checksum; SHA-256 is slower to compute but is the one specified in a formal cryptographic standard. Either one catches a copy that landed wrong.

What does habitat flow do better?

habitat flow has a Free plan that covers the whole offload process up to 150 GB a week, with up to three folder presets — genuinely free, not a time-boxed trial. Its Pro plan can mirror a card to two drives in one pass, and its companion iPhone app shows live ingest status and pushes a notification when a copy is verified, so you can step away from the Mac while it works. Pro can also be bought once, for $149, instead of a yearly $69 subscription, if you would rather own it outright.

What does MediaFlowSwift do better?

habitat flow's job is the offload: it does not categorize footage into a project structure, review or rate clips, archive to numbered drives, write reports, or help with publishing. MediaFlowSwift does all of that after the same kind of checksum-proved copy: it files each clip into Category and Camera folders, offers frame-accurate review with star ratings and tags, archives to numbered drives with a record of what went where, and writes PDF, HTML and CSV reports. Studio Pro also drafts YouTube titles, descriptions, chapters and tags from the finished video. If the card is only the start of the work, that is the gap.

Which one should you pick?

If the job stops at a verified offload — a light shoot, a short trip, a budget that starts at zero — habitat flow's Free plan may be all you need. If you want the same proved copy to turn automatically into an organized, reviewed, reported and archived project, without a second app for the rest of the pipeline, that is what MediaFlowSwift is for.
