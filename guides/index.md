# Guides

Short, practical answers to the questions that come up when you move footage off a card on a Mac. Each guide answers one question in its first paragraph. Then it says what MediaFlowSwift does, step by step, and where it stops.

The guides are grouped by the moment you need them. That means before you erase a card, when something looks wrong, when the drives pile up, while you review, and when you publish. If you only read one, read the first. It covers the mistake that costs footage.

## Before you erase a card

- **[Is It Safe to Erase Your Card After Importing on a Mac?](/guides/verify-footage-copied-before-erasing-card/)**
  Checksums, not playback, prove a copy is safe. What gets checked at import, at organize and before Clear Card, and what never does.

- **[How to Keep Two Checked Copies of a Memory Card on a Mac](/guides/two-checked-copies-of-a-card-on-a-mac/)**
  Import and organize to your working drive, then archive to a second. Two checked copies, made one after the other, not at the same time.

## When something looks wrong

- **[Footage Missing After Moving to an External Drive?](/guides/footage-missing-after-moving-to-external-drive/)**
  Your clips aren't lost. Library Moved repoints every project, and checks each file before it changes anything. Nothing is copied or deleted.

## Keeping a shelf of drives straight

- **[How to Automate Video File Organization on Your Mac](/guides/automate-video-file-organization-on-mac/)**
  Category and Camera folders, sorted every time, with Workflow Templates to chain the routine into one command.

- **[How to Label and Catalog Your Archive Drives for Video](/guides/label-and-catalog-archive-drives-for-video/)**
  Every archive drive gets a number, and the app keeps the record of what went where. One sticker per drive, not a spreadsheet.

## Reviewing, finding and handing off footage

- **[How to Review Footage and Pick Your Selects on a Mac](/guides/review-and-pick-selects-on-a-mac/)**
  Rapid Review plays, rates and rejects clips by keyboard. Smart Selects scores each one first, so you review in quality order.

- **[How to Find a Clip by What Was Said in It on a Mac](/guides/search-video-by-what-was-said/)**
  Transcribe your footage on-device, then search every transcript at once for a word or phrase.

- **[How to Create a Dailies Report and a Hand-Off File](/guides/dailies-report-and-hand-off-file-for-editor/)**
  A by-day report for a director to read, and a separate export for your editor's software.

- **[How to Find Where Each Clip in Your Project Was Filmed](/guides/find-where-footage-was-filmed-shoot-map/)**
  The Shoot Map pins every clip with GPS data by location, clustered and clickable. It also covers which clips have GPS data, and what the map sends to Apple.

## What the guides assume

- **Your Mac:** MediaFlowSwift runs on macOS 14 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon.
- **Your plan:** most guides work on either plan. YouTube publishing and the database server several Macs share need Studio Pro.
- **Your storage:** cards, external drives and shared drives you already own.
- **Your privacy:** features that send anything off your Mac, such as maps or place names, are off until you turn them on. Settings › Privacy lists each one.
