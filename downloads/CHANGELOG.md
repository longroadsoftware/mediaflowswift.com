# MediaFlowSwift Release Notes

What's new in each version of MediaFlowSwift, newest first.

## 1.10.49 · October 18, 2026

**Camera timecode in your edits, checked iPhone imports, and safer updates and archives.**

- **Your camera's timecode, in Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve.** Clips from cameras that record timecode, such as Sony, Canon and Blackmagic, now start at that timecode in your exports, drop frame or not, just as the camera recorded it.
- **iPhone imports are checked like card imports.** Every clip copied from your iPhone is now checked against the phone, and the import tells you when all of them are safe.
- **Audio Sync for projects of any size.** Line up your sound recorder with your cameras, however many clips the project has.
- **Safer updates, with Revert.** A new version is checked before it replaces the one you have, and Revert always tells you which version it will put back.
- **Archives you can always bring back.** Your original stays until its archive is complete and checked, and a split archive shows which drive holds which files.
- **Hero, everywhere.** Favorites is now Hero throughout the app: the F key, the sidebar, the selection bar and the right-click menu.
- **Bug fixes.** Fixes for saving, sharing a project between Macs, iPhone imports, Grid view and licence checks, plus small improvements throughout.
