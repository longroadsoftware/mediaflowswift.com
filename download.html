Being prepared

Download for Mac

Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs are not supported yet. Versions for Intel Macs and for Windows are coming: get an email when yours is ready.
  • Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without a fuss.
  • No account, no sign-in. The app never contacts a server of ours except to check for new versions and, once you enter a key, to check your licence; the privacy page lists exactly what is sent.

Installing

  1. Double-click the downloaded zip in Finder to unzip it (Safari may have done this for you), and drag MediaFlowswift into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications. The 14-day trial of Studio Pro starts the first time it opens.
  3. If you use a network share or a database server on your network, macOS asks once whether the app may use your local network. Say yes; the manual explains why.

Won’t open, or macOS says it is damaged? See what to do when MediaFlowSwift won’t open.
