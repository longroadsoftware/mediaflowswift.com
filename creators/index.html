A free year of MediaFlowSwift for a few honest creators

Mike and Sheri run On Island Time RV. They built MediaFlowSwift to manage their own RV footage — cards from a few cameras, every trip, no account, nothing deleted until it’s proved safe.

Now we’re looking for a few small creators who shoot real footage on a Mac to use it for a year, free, and tell us honestly what works and what doesn’t.

Spots are limited. We read every application and pick creators who are a good fit — not everyone who applies will be chosen.

What you get

  • One year of Studio Pro, free. The normal 3-Mac limit applies.
  • After the year, your plan turns read-only, the same as any ended plan. Your footage is never locked.

What we ask in return

  • Use it on at least one real project in your first 30 days.
  • Answer two short feedback emails, around day 30 and day 90.
  • Honest answers — good or bad.

We don’t ask for a review, a video, a mention, or a rating. If you choose to talk about it publicly, just say you got it free.
